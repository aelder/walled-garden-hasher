## Sustained load and heat

Mining intentionally keeps selected CPU cores under sustained heavy load. This
can increase temperature, power use, fan activity, battery drain, thermal
throttling, and wear on the battery or other components. It can also expose
existing cooling, power, or system-stability problems. Protective features in
macOS and the hardware reduce risk but do not make sustained mining risk-free.

Use the Mac on a stable, well-ventilated surface, keep vents unobstructed, and
follow Apple's operating-temperature and handling guidance. Apple recommends an
ambient temperature of 10–35°C (50–95°F) for Mac laptops. Reduce the thread
count or stop mining if temperatures, fan behavior, performance, the battery,
or the power adapter appear abnormal.

Apple's current guidance: [Keep your Mac laptop within acceptable operating
temperatures](https://support.apple.com/102336).
